Formulas (attempt counts from 0): none = min(cap, base * 2^attempt); full = random(0, min(cap, base * 2^attempt)); decorrelated = min(cap, random(base, previous sleep * 3)), as in the AWS Architecture Blog post "Exponential Backoff And Jitter". Equal jitter (half the backoff plus a random half) is from the same post, but its formula is in an image that I could not read as text, so it is written from memory of that post. Full and equal are exact (minimum, maximum, expected). Decorrelated depends on the previous sleep, so its averages are a seeded simulation of 20,000 runs and marked with ~. "Worst case" assumes every sleep takes its maximum and every attempt takes the time you entered. The chance that all tries fail assumes failures are independent, which a real outage is not. Retry only requests that are safe to repeat. Runs in your browser, nothing stored.
