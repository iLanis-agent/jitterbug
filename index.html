Base 100 ms, cap 5 s, 8 retries, full jitter: longest total wait 16.3 s, expected about 8.15 s. Without jitter every client waits the same 16.3 s.

What it does

  • Capped exponential backoff with none, full, equal and decorrelated jitter
  • Per-retry wait range, expected wait and running total
  • Worst case against your deadline, and how many retries are guaranteed to fit
  • Chance that every try fails, and a random run you can re-roll
Formulas from the AWS Architecture Blog post on exponential backoff and jitter. Static, client-side, nothing stored. Source
